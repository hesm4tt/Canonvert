Formats & exports

Digify accepts supported HEIF/HEIC images and DNG/Apple ProRAW files from Photos or Files. RAW development depends on iOS and the source file being supported by Apple's imaging frameworks.

Choose the Everyday, Flash, or Night look, then export as HEIC (a HEIF format) or JPEG. Digify renders at the source image's dimensions and uses the highest available encoder quality. The selected look is baked into the exported pixels.

The ELPH 360 HS A captures JPEG rather than RAW. Digify develops supported RAW files on-device and applies an inspired rendering; it does not recreate Canon's proprietary processing pipeline.

Metadata

Digify carries supported source metadata into exports, which can include capture date and time, GPS location, orientation, and original camera make/model. Availability depends on the input format and what iOS exposes. Exported image dimensions are not intentionally reduced.

The app identifies its processing software in the output metadata where supported. It does not replace the source camera identity with Canon.

Privacy

Photo selection, processing, and export happen on your device. Digify does not upload your photos to a Digify server. You choose which image to open and where to save the result.

When using the Files picker, iOS grants access to the selected item. The app needs photo-library access only for the actions you choose. No account is required.

Digify is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Canon Inc.

Compatibility

Digify is an iOS app distributed as an unsigned IPA. A compatible iPhone, a recent iOS version supported by the app build, and a personal sideloading/signing setup are needed. The app targets iOS 16 and later. Device compatibility also depends on the sideloading tool and signing setup you use.

SideStore and LiveContainer have their own device and setup requirements. Refer to their official documentation for current instructions.
